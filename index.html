Featured Native Plant

Purple Coneflower

Scientific Name: Echinacea purpurea

Description: A hardy perennial wildflower famous for its bright purple petals that gracefully droop away from a prominent, spiky brownish-orange central cone.

  • Native Range: Eastern and central North America
  • Sunlight Requirements: Full sun to partial shade
  • Soil Requirements: Well-drained, average to loamy soil
  • Water Requirements: Low to moderate (drought-tolerant once established)
  • Blooming Season: Summer to early autumn (June to September)
  • Wildlife Benefits: Highly attractive to native bees, butterflies, and goldfinches that feed on the seeds.
  • Plant Height: 2 to 4 feet tall

For additional botanical details, visit the Lady Bird Johnson Wildflower Center.
